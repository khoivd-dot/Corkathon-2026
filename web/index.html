        What this can’t tell you
        • We can’t see your container. A problem on your route may or may not affect it, so the next step is always your forwarder.
        • News is a signal, not proof. We only count headlines that name a port on your route and a disruption, but quiet delays may never be reported.
        • Official confirmation is switched off until the reuse terms of carrier and port notices are recorded, so today every event shows as “reported”. We link the notices of the ports we can’t check.
        • The exchange rate is the ECB reference rate, published once a working day. It isn’t your bank’s rate and says nothing about delays.
        • Ship positions aren’t shown: we haven’t confirmed the right to display vessel-tracking data.
        • Official trade figures are two to three months old, so we use them as background only.
